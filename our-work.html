Impact Story

From reactive care to early prevention

In one partner region, community animal health workers were spending most of their time responding to outbreaks after they had already spread through a herd. Working with local veterinary teams, we introduced a simple mobile reporting tool so early symptoms could be logged and shared the same day, instead of waiting for a scheduled visit.

Within the first season, response times to reported cases dropped sharply, and the same reporting data began informing where mobile veterinary clinics were scheduled next — turning a reactive system into a preventative one.
