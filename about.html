About DASOWF Africa

A Pan-African, non-governmental, non-profit foundation transforming African animal production through digital innovation and One Welfare.

Our Story

Founded on a connected view of welfare

DASOWF Africa was established by a founding group who recognised that the productivity and welfare of animals are inextricably linked to human wellbeing and environmental health across Africa — and that digital innovation is the most powerful catalyst for a sustainable, equitable, science-driven transformation of the continent's animal production systems. The Foundation's Constitution was adopted by its Founding Members on 5 May 2026 in Kumasi.

As a Pan-African, non-governmental, non-profit organisation, DASOWF Africa works across livestock and aquaculture value chains — helping farmers, veterinarians, extension workers and agribusinesses adopt digital tools at a pace and scale traditional programs alone can't reach.

Our Mission

To accelerate the adoption of digital technologies across African animal production value chains, integrating One Welfare principles — the interconnectedness of animal welfare, human wellbeing, and environmental health — to achieve lasting sustainability and productivity gains.

Our Vision

A digitally transformed African animal production sector that is sustainable, welfare-centred, climate-resilient, and capable of delivering food and nutrition security for all.

Our Guiding Principles

IN

Inclusivity

Ensuring the digital transition benefits reach smallholder farmers, women, youth and marginalised communities without exception.

SI

Scientific Integrity

Grounding all programmes and publications in rigorous, peer-reviewed evidence.

OW

One Welfare

Recognising that animal health, human wellbeing and environmental sustainability must be advanced together.

TA

Transparency & Accountability

Maintaining open governance, accurate financial reporting and regular public communication of outcomes.

PA

Pan-African Identity

Celebrating Africa's diversity while fostering regional integration and South-South collaboration.

Governance

The Foundation is governed by a General Assembly (the supreme decision-making body of all registered members, convening at least annually), a Board of Trustees of 5–11 elected members responsible for strategic oversight and financial stewardship, a Secretariat headed by an Executive Director appointed by the Board, and Advisory & Technical Committees providing expert guidance on digital innovation, animal welfare and research.
