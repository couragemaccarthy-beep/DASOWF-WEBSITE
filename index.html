What We Do

Four pillars of our work

Drawn directly from our founding objectives, every program advances one or more of these priorities.

01

Digital Transformation

Promoting AI, IoT, big data, precision livestock farming and digital traceability across livestock and aquaculture value chains.

02

One Welfare Integration

Advancing animal welfare, human wellbeing and environmental sustainability as interdependent goals.

03

Digital Capacity Building

Training smallholder farmers, veterinarians, extension workers and agribusiness operators.

04

Research & Partnerships

Publishing evidence-based research and building alliances that scale digital solutions across Africa.

Programs

Where we're making an impact

A sample of the initiatives driving measurable change on the ground.

01

Precision Livestock Farming

IoT sensors and data tools that help farmers monitor herd health, feeding and production in real time.

02

Digital Traceability Systems

Farm-to-market traceability for livestock and aquaculture, built for Africa's diverse agro-ecological contexts.

03

Farmer & Extension Digital Capacity

Training and mentorship platforms for smallholder farmers, veterinarians and extension workers.
